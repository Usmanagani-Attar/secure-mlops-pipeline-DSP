# Security Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood (1-5) | Impact (1-5) | Risk Score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Invalid prediction input | 3 | 3 | 9 |
| Data validation failure | 3 | 4 | 12 |
| Hardcoded secrets | 3 | 5 | 15 |
| Vulnerable dependency | 4 | 4 | 16 |
| Insecure container execution | 3 | 4 | 12 |

Risk score:

R = Likelihood × Impact

The values are academic project assessment values used to demonstrate the risk model and should be updated if the actual testing produces different evidence.
